# System Audit — June 2026

Complete audit of the Autonomous Multi-Vertical Advertising Engine against
2026 industry standards, plus the build sprint that followed it (Waves 1–6,
all in this repo's history dated 2026-06-11). Owner decisions that scoped the
sprint: full build sprint; Phase Two restriction lifted; research all media
buying channels and build stub adapters for the top 2–3.

## 1. Current-state scorecard vs. industry standard

| Area | Industry standard (2026) | This system | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Server-side conversions | Server-side API relay with dedup is table stakes; browser-only tracking loses 30–50% of attribution | Conversion hub: idempotent ingestion on (source_site, event_id), 5-platform relay (Meta CAPI, Google enhanced conversions, TikTok Events, Snap CAPI, Pinterest events), retries + dead-letter + replay | **At/above standard** |
| Event match quality | 8+ hashed identifiers/event; EMQ ≥6 target; advertisers see 15–25% better CPA at high EMQ (dataally.ai, adsgo.ai) | 8 hashed identifier slots + IP/UA auto-enrichment + click IDs; EMQ-style 0–10 signal score per site in Settings; live EMQ from Meta's Dataset Quality API pending P5 credentials | **At standard** (W2) |
| Consent handling | Consent Mode v2 mandatory in EEA; recovers 30–70% of consent-lost conversions via modeling (didomi.io) | `ad_user_data`/`ad_personalization` carried in the contract and passed through to Google; consent-denied events never relay | **At standard** (W2) |
| Click-ID capture | First-party landing-page capture with ≥90-day persistence | `/pixel.js` snippet: 7 click-ID params, 180-day persistence, auto ViewContent, `adEngine.track()` | **At standard** (W2) |
| Creative velocity | 15–30+ variants/month built on hook frameworks; creative is the targeting (fospha.com, madgicx.com) | 6 hook archetypes × 3 formats per generation run, predicted scoring, fatigue rotation, capped at 72 variant-rows/7d per product | **At standard** (W3) |
| Creative learning loop | Per-variant performance feeds the next brief | Daily playbook-priors job credits conversions to launched hook types per vertical; generation orders hooks by priors | **At standard** (W3) |
| Lead-gen optimization | Optimize toward closed revenue (offline conversions), not form fills — the single biggest lead-gen lever | `/v1/leads/outcome`: closed leads become canonical purchases inheriting the lead's identity, relayed as offline conversions; funnel shows Closed row | **At standard** (W3) |
| Net-return optimization | ROAS alone is considered insufficient; blended cost and margin-aware value are differentiators | Net return = revenue ÷ (ad spend + operating cost) everywhere; per-product margin % refines value | **Above standard** |
| Incrementality | Geo holdout is the gold standard; 2–4 week windows, quarterly re-runs (measured.com, segmentstream.com) | Experiments table + endpoints; lift % computed from observed readouts; feeds the Overview incremental tile; runbook documents the method | **At standard, manual readouts** (W3) |
| Safety/guardrails | Spend caps and human gates expected of autonomous buying tools | Guardrail-by-construction (unforgeable approval objects), DB-flag kill switch, insert-only Decision/CostEvent ledger, deterministic fast loop, restricted-category sign-off | **Above standard** — most commercial tools rely on convention, not construction |
| Channels | Meta+Google+TikTok is the SMB baseline; best-in-class tools add 2–4 secondary channels | 5 channels (Meta, Google, TikTok, Snapchat, Pinterest), stub-mode drivers, identical guardrail path | **At standard** (W4) |
| Notifications | Email/push on approval, errors, weekly summary | Outbox + Resend-backed email adapter: attention alerts, sign-off confirmation, weekly digest | **At standard** (W5) |
| Security | Authenticated admin surface, escaped output | OPERATOR_TOKEN gate on `/internal/*`, HTML-escaped hosted pages, one-time site keys | **Fixed in W1** (was a gap) |

## 2. Research findings (sources)

- **AI media buying landscape, creative velocity baselines**: hyperfx.ai,
  adstellar.ai, madgicx.com, get-ryze.ai — autonomous tools converge on one
  broad campaign + high-velocity hook-framework creative; 15–30+ variants/mo
  is the working baseline; predicted creative scoring pre-spend is standard.
- **EMQ / Conversions API**: dataally.ai, adsgo.ai, developers.facebook.com,
  growthspreeofficial.com — 8+ identifiers per event, EMQ ≥6 target,
  15–25% CPA improvement at high match quality; Dataset Quality API exposes
  live EMQ per event type.
- **Consent Mode v2**: didomi.io — required for EEA traffic on Google;
  conversion modeling recovers 30–70% of consent-lost attribution.
- **Smart+ / Advantage+ practice**: fospha.com, lunio.ai, chriscurry.co.uk —
  consolidate to one AI campaign per product/goal; the creative filters the
  audience; account simplification beats granular structures.
- **Click-to-WhatsApp in UAE/GCC**: messagecentral.com, go4whatsup.com,
  pickyassist.com — dominant SMB performance channel in the region; CPL
  25–45% lower than landing-page funnels; 3–8× ROAS reported; 72h free
  messaging window after a CTWA tap.
- **Channel landscape**: emarketer.com, attekmi.com, cometly.com,
  freshpaint.io, learn.microsoft.com — CTV $40B+ and retail media growing
  double digits; server-side conversion APIs now exist for Snapchat,
  Pinterest, LinkedIn, Reddit, and X, making conversion-hub fan-out cheap to
  extend.
- **Incrementality**: measured.com, segmentstream.com, emarketer.com — geo
  holdouts are the credible standard; 2–4 week windows; platform-reported
  conversions systematically overstate true lift.

## 4. What the sprint built (by wave)

1. **W1 Security**: operator key on `/internal/*` (+ Unlock screen); hosted
   pages HTML-escaped (XSS fixed); in-UI site onboarding with one-time keys.
2. **W2 Measurement**: contract v2 (8 hashed identifiers, IP/UA, Consent
   Mode v2, sccid/epik); relay mappings enriched; per-site signal-strength
   score (0–10) in Settings; `/pixel.js` browser snippet; integration docs
   rewritten around it.
3. **W3 Outcomes**: lead-quality loop (closed leads relay as purchases);
   6-hook creative framework + self-updating playbook priors; measured
   WhatsApp destination (CTWA-lite); margin-aware net return; geo-holdout
   experiments feeding the incremental tile.
4. **W4 Channels**: Snapchat + Pinterest campaign adapters and conversion
   relays in stub mode, 5-platform fan-out, env/deploy wiring, P5 extended.
5. **W5 UX**: notifications (outbox + email adapter + scheduler dispatch);
   first-run checklist on the empty Overview; creative format preview tabs.
6. **W6**: this report + governance updates (CLAUDE.md annotation, BACKLOG.md,
   DECISIONS.md).

All waves landed with `pnpm check` green (typecheck, lint, ui-strings lint,
mutation-path audit, 140+ tests) and e2e suites re-baselined; `e2e:full` now
exercises the lead-closed loop and 5-platform relay.

## 5. Forward roadmap (not built, prioritized)

1. **Live-mode soak (P1–P6 in BLOCKED.md)** — everything below is worth less
   than getting real credentials into staging and validating recorded payload
   shapes against live APIs.
2. **Live EMQ via Meta Dataset Quality API** — replace the proxy score with
   platform-reported EMQ once P5 lands; alert on EMQ <6.
3. **LTV-based conversion values** — needs external_id adoption from sites;
   then value = predicted LTV, not first-purchase value.
4. **Server-side tag gateway** — GTM server container for sites that can't
   paste a script tag.
5. **LinkedIn channel** (if any B2B vertical is onboarded) — same adapter
   seam as W4.
6. **Comment management** — brand-safety reply/hide automation; needs
   moderation policy decisions.
7. **Competitor monitoring** — ad-library ingestion to inform creative
   briefs.
8. **Fraud gating** — IP/UA anomaly scoring at ingestion beyond dedup.
9. **Multi-tenancy** — accounts, roles, per-tenant data isolation; currently
   a single-operator deployment behind OPERATOR_TOKEN.
10. **Arabic/RTL localization** — significant UAE upside; full design pass.

## 6. Honest limitations

- All five platform drivers run in **stub mode** until P5 credentials exist;
  payload shapes are recorded best-effort from public docs and must be
  verified against live APIs (noted in BLOCKED.md).
- Incrementality math runs on operator-entered readouts; region-level spend
  attribution is not automated.
- The signal-strength score is a local proxy, not platform-reported EMQ.
- Weekly digest and alert emails depend on OPERATOR_EMAIL being set; in stub
  mode they land in the outbox/logs only.

# Addendum — round 2 (video, competitor teardown, rules)

Owner direction: "video works better than images"; reviewed bir.ch.

- **Video data (2026):** video ≈58% of Meta budgets; short-form ≈ half of all digital video spend; video CPMs 15–40% lower (largest on Reels); short-form CTR ~58% higher; statics still win retargeting CPA (~34% lower) → mixed ~70/30 video-to-image accounts see ~19% higher returns. Sources: IAB 2026 Digital Video report (via thekeyword.co), stackmatix.com, attnagency.com, velacore.au, saasultra.com.
- **Birch/Revealbot (bir.ch):** automation rules builder, bulk launcher, creative insights explorer, reports, tracking gateway; Meta/Google/TikTok/Snap; $2B managed. Parity closed this round: user-defined rules (through our guardrails) + per-ad insights. Sources: staymodern.ai, linktly.com, topsocialtools.com.
- **AdAmigo:** 3-agent "AI media buyer" (action/creative/chat), approve-or-auto-execute, $99–349/mo/account; Meta Andromeda "creative-as-targeting" era makes creative diversity the primary lever. Structural parity already held; chat ops → BACKLOG. Sources: adamigo.ai, tryatria.com, marketingbrew.com.
- **UGC video tools (Arcads $110+, Creatify $39+, ClipLoft $49+):** batch 10 video variants in minutes. Closed this round in stub: per-concept 15s 9:16 videos with hook-tagged scene scripts; live provider choice remains P4.

Built in round 2 (W9): video-first creative pipeline (generation + uploads + previews), per-ad insights with winning-hook leaderboard, Birch-style custom automation rules executed through the guardrail layer.
